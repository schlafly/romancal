Replace single-kernel source detection with a bank of matched filters.  The
image is convolved with templates spanning a range of source sizes, each
convolution is turned into a significance image, and the per-pixel maximum
over templates becomes a single detection image on which sources are found
and deblended once.  The templates are built to sum to zero, so none of them
responds to a smooth background and a compact source sitting on a large
galaxy's light is measured by its prominence above that light rather than by
the light itself.  ``snr_threshold`` now means the peak significance in
sigma, the same for every template, and ``npixels`` is the minimum size of a
deblended child.  Two columns are added to the catalog: ``det_template``,
which template detected each source, and ``det_significance``, its peak
significance.  The bad-pixel mask is now passed to the background estimator
as ``mask`` rather than ``coverage_mask``, which had left the background RMS,
and so the detection threshold, undefined at masked pixels -- most of which
are the saturated cores of the brightest sources.
